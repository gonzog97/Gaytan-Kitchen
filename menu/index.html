Menu

Breakfast served all day

Plus lunch, dinner, and a self-serve case of fresh-baked goods. Prices below are drawn from Gaytan Kitchen's posted weekly menu — always ask in-house for the current daily special.

Breakfast

$8 – $18

Served all day. Chilaquiles, biscuits and gravy, breakfast burritos, pancakes, French toast, breakfast buns stuffed with bacon or chorizo, and chicken fried steak with eggs and hashbrowns.

Sandwiches

$13 – $15

French dip, BBQ pulled pork, BLT, club, and grilled ham and cheese — classic American lunch, made from scratch, served with the salad of the day.

Gaytán's Specialties

$11 – $18

Quesabirria tacos, pupusas with curtido and salsa, chicken fried steak, and a build-your-own salad bar — the dishes reviewers ask for by name.

Bakery & Grab-and-Go

From $8

Cinnamon rolls (a customer favorite — 9 separate reviews call them out by name), breakfast buns, banana bread, and a rotating self-serve case of pies, flan, and cheesecake.

How to Order

Order ahead, or come sit down

Most customers call ahead for easy pickup, but you're always welcome to dine in and enjoy your meal here.

  • 1. Call (509) 398-5530 or stop by — 905 E St SW, Quincy, WA
  • 2. We prepare your order fresh, from scratch
  • 3. Dine in, pick up, or grab something from the self-serve case
  • 4. Open Thu–Sat 8am–5:30pm, Sun 8am–2pm

Hosting an event?

Our kitchen is available for private events and custom catering — from a family gathering to a full pupusa fiesta.
